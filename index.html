AI PhD Researcher at Google Research, NYC (2025). Investigating geometric memory in Transformers and next-token prediction objectives.

Research
My research focuses on interpretable representation learning for temporal data with applications in healthcare — developing methodology that uncovers how evolving patient health status is encoded over time. I am also interested in how the next-token prediction paradigm shapes the strengths and limitations of large language models, and how sequence models exhibit implicit geometric forms of memory beyond simple associative recall. Additionally, I work on causal inference and survival analysis for high-stakes healthcare decision-making.
